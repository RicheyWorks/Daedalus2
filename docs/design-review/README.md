## What is ready to discuss

The proposal connects the existing shattered-planet story to a clear player motivation: sustain a home, discover a rainbow, enter another kind of game and return with knowledge and earned rewards. Your unique rainbow trails, realm-specific items, unicorn groups, burning-bull fear, mountain alpha, twelve-unicorn chest and quest-giving unicorns are preserved. The existing Dungeon Master project belongs inside the Labyrinth realm.

New lore, names, act order, ordinary unicorn quest rewards and legendary item abilities are explicitly proposed. The impact remains the established cause of the shattered world; no secret villain or new cosmology is silently declared canon.
