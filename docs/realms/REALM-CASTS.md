# Realm casts: who lives past the rainbows

Status: proposal from unreal-team, 2026-10-13. Richey asked for "tons of characters" and "lots of npc". Astra
(team-codex) owns the split and the Blender build. All names, looks and roles are original. Story frame: the
planet shattered (ADR-029). Each rainbow leads through a sky gate (ADR-031) to a realm that kept one piece of the
old world's knowledge, and each realm holds a Heartstone to heal the planet.

Per realm: 1 hero form, 10+ NPC roles, 12 creatures across rarity tiers (common, uncommon, rare, elite, boss),
3 mounts or companions, 4 pets. A role becomes a character when its silhouette differs, not just its palette.
Every NPC gets one job the player can use (a shop, a quest, a ride, a hint, a trade) and one line of gossip about
another realm, so the realms point at each other.

## Gallery City (mount: drone)
A city where the paintings are alive and the streets are hung frames. Its knowledge is colour and light.

Hero form: **the Sketcher**. Charcoal coat, a palette shield, a brush staff that paints platforms for a moment.

NPCs:
- **Curator Vell**: tall and thin, wears frame-shaped spectacles. Gives the Heartstone quest.
- **Frame-Hanger twins**: they move whole buildings on pulleys and open new streets each day.
- **Varnish Monk**: protects art from fading. Sells shine potions.
- **Restorer Ama**: fixes broken murals. Each fixed panel tells part of the Shattering.
- **Pigment Miner**: digs colour ore under the city. Teaches crafting.
- **Gallery Guard (clockwork)**: polite, very strict, lets you in after closing for a bribe of paint.
- **Street Muralist kid**: tags walls with hints to secrets.
- **Light Tailor**: dyes armour. Cosmetic shop.
- **Drone Mechanic Pell**: repairs and upgrades the drone mount.
- **Critic Bloom**: rates your builds and gives rewards for good ones.
- **Lost Self-Portrait**: an NPC who stepped out of his painting and can't find his frame. Side quest.
- **Echo Choir**: three statues that sing the jukebox cue for the district you are in.

Creatures:
- Common: **Smudgelings**, little ink blobs that steal colour; **Thumbtack Beetles**; **Canvas Moths**.
- Uncommon: **Easel Striders** on three legs; **Turpentine Toads**.
- Rare: **Mosaic Hound**, which falls apart into tiles and reforms; **Gilded Frame Mimic**.
- Elite: **Sentinel Bust**, a giant marble head that rolls; **Chromatic Wraith**, which swaps your colours.
- Boss: **The Grey Wash**, a living fog that drains all colour from the city.
- Ambient: **Paper Cranes** (flocks) and **Brushtail Squirrels**.

Mounts and companions: drone (the realm mount), the **Kite Ray**, which glides over rooftops, and the **Easel Mule**.

Pets: the **Inkpup**, a **Prism Finch**, a **Crayon Gecko** and a **Lamp Moth**.

## The Parcels (mount: helicopter)
Patchwork land of fenced plots, mail routes and farm suburbs. Its knowledge is trade and growing.

Hero form: **the Courier**. Satchel armour, a stamp hammer, a parcel that is also a shield.

NPCs:
- **Postmaster Grue**: runs the delivery board, which works as the quest system.
- **Plot Surveyor Ilka**: sells land claims (ties to deeds, ADR-027).
- **Fence Smith**: walls, gates and locks for your plot.
- **Seed Broker Ott**: a rare seed trader whose stock changes with the weather.
- **Scarecrow Elder**: wise and slow. Knows the old field songs.
- **Helipad Chief Rosa**: helicopter rides and upgrades.
- **Milk Cart Twins**: deliver goods between plots. Escort quests.
- **Lost-Letter Ghost**: wants undelivered mail delivered to people in other realms.
- **Beekeeper Hollis**: pollination quests and honey.
- **Auction Crow**: a giant crow that runs the market.
- **Weather Vane Keeper**: predicts storms, which change crop yields.
- **Neighbour Feud pair**: two farmers in a boundary dispute. Pick a side.

Creatures:
- Common: **Mailbox Mimics**, **Burrow Voles**, **Hay Sprites**.
- Uncommon: **Thistle Boars** and **Twine Snakes**.
- Rare: **Wheelbarrow Golem**; **Stampede Calf**, which is harmless until the herd joins in.
- Elite: **Combine Crab**, a harvester-shaped crab; **Rustwing Hawk**.
- Boss: **The Blight Bailiff**, who repossesses plots and spreads rot.
- Ambient: **Fence Larks** and **Puddle Ducks**.

Mounts and companions: helicopter (the realm mount), the **Draft Ox**, and the **Gyro Goat**, which can climb fences.

Pets: the **Parcel Pup** (fetches deliveries), the **Clover Rabbit**, the **Barn Owlet** and the **Seed Mouse**.

## Brick Realm (mount: plane)
Everything is built from snap-together bricks, and anything can be taken apart. Its knowledge is building.

Hero form: **the Builder**. Brick plate armour, a stud hammer, and a backpack that snaps on new parts.

NPCs:
- **Foreman Kip**: blueprints quests (build a bridge, a tower).
- **Brick Baker**: kilns bricks. Sells rare colours.
- **Blueprint Archivist**: a library of designs. Unlocks recipes.
- **Crane Operator Sal**: lifts big builds. Moves your structures.
- **Demolition Duo**: two NPCs who knock things down. Challenge races.
- **Mayor Minifold**: a tiny mayor in a big hat. City politics quests.
- **Airfield Ace Nova**: plane rides, upgrades and races.
- **Toy Soldier Sergeant**: trains you for squad tactics (ties to tactics).
- **Missing-Piece Collector**: wants rare bricks from every realm.
- **Glue Witch**: makes things permanent, for a price.
- **Studmonger**: currency exchange.
- **Builder Kids Club**: four kids who build forts and hide clues in them.

Creatures:
- Common: **Brickbugs**, **Loose Studs** (bouncing), **Plate Crabs**.
- Uncommon: **Hinge Hounds** and **Slope Slugs**.
- Rare: **Rebuild Rex**, a dino that reassembles into new shapes; **Wind-up Knight**.
- Elite: **Tower Titan**, a walking tower; **Magnet Moth**, which pulls bricks off you.
- Boss: **The Unmaker**, a giant hand that dismantles the city.
- Ambient: **Clockwork Pigeons** and **Spring Frogs**.

Mounts and companions: plane (the realm mount), the **Brick Pony** (rebuildable), and the **Hover Cart**.

Pets: the **Studpup**, the **Tile Turtle**, the **Spinner Top** (a living top) and the **Pocket Bot**.

## Labyrinth (mount: sky-strider)
The maze realm where Daedalus began: walls that move at night. Its knowledge is ways and paths.

Hero form: **the Wayfinder**. Thread spool, chalk, and a compass that hums near exits.

NPCs:
- **Thread Spinner Ari**: sells thread that marks your path.
- **Wallwright**: rebuilds the maze each night. Bribable.
- **Lantern Keeper**: lights paths and sells light.
- **Lost Cartographer**: sells partial maps, and some of them are lies.
- **Echo Hermit**: answers questions by echo, only in dead ends.
- **Gate Riddler**: opens doors for riddles.
- **Strider Tamer Oona**: bonds you with the sky-strider bird.
- **Statue Gardener**: tends hedge walls that grow back.
- **Herd Warden Tobi**: keeps the maze cattle (the **Hornbacks**, an original creature) calm.
- **Trapsmith**: disarms traps or sells them.
- **Twin Doors**: one lies, one tells the truth.
- **The Architect's Apprentice**: knows why the planet shattered. Late-game story NPC.

Creatures:
- Common: **Corner Rats**, **Chalk Eaters** (they erase your marks), **Glow Grubs**.
- Uncommon: **Hedge Stalkers** and **Mirror Bats**.
- Rare: **Wall Mimic**; **Thread Spider**, which cuts your thread.
- Elite: **Hornback Bull** (stampedes down corridors); **Sentinel Sphinxling**.
- Boss: **The Folding Maze**, a creature that is the maze itself.
- Ambient: **Moss Snails** and **Echo Crickets**.

Mounts and companions: sky-strider (the realm mount), the **Corridor Hound**, and the **Lantern Wisp**.

Pets: the **Chalk Kitten**, the **Compass Beetle**, the **Mazemouse** and the **Thread Moth**.

## The 8-bit realm, upgraded (ADR-052)
Keep the NES feel, but make the cast sharper. Each sprite gets a crisp silhouette, a 4-frame walk, an attack pose, a
hurt pose and a palette swap tier.

Hero party: **Knight, Tinker, Herbalist, Bard**. Your team changes into these forms (Richey's ask).

Town NPCs:
- **Innkeeper Rusk**
- **Smith Garra**
- **Item Shop Pim**
- **the Queen of Cinders**
- **Royal Scribe** (the save point)
- **Fortune Teller**
- **Keymaker**
- **Well Child** (gives hints)
- **Night Watch**
- **Retired Hero** (side quest)
- **Ferry Woman**
- **Tavern Bard** (who plays jukebox cues)

Creatures, each with a palette-swap ladder of 3 tiers:
- Common: **Blight Slime**, **Puffcap**, **Dust Wisp**, **Stone Knight**, **Shardback**.
- Additional common: **Mudgulp**, **Bat Lantern**.
- Rare: **Moon Hare**, which flees, and **Chest Mimic**.
- Elite: **Bone Drake** and **Iron Golem**.
- Boss: **Shard Tyrant**.
- The lucky drip: **Golden Puffcap**, about once in fifty fights.

## Cross-realm NPCs (travel the gates, appear everywhere)
- **Gate Ferryman Bram** (already on Orr): rides with you through rainbows. The story guide.
- **Rainbow Painter**: repaints faded rainbows. A quest per gate.
- **Wandering Merchant Zee**: the stock depends on the last realm visited.
- **Heartstone Seekers**, a rival party of 4: they race you to each realm's Heartstone.
- **Courier of Worlds**: carries items between realms for a fee.
- **The Tugboat Balloonist**: tows islands to new spots with an air-balloon tug (Richey's ask). Owners hire her to
  move their NFT island.
