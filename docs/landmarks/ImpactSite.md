# Asteroid impact site (story landmark)

Story: the asteroid hit, and the arks save life. This is where it fell.

- Marker: `landmark_impact_site`, Wild Biomes edge
- Crater: 120 m wide and 25 m deep, with a scorched rim, glassy floor and shattered rock rays
- Core: `Prop_Asteroid_Core` (8 m, half buried, crystal veins that pulse faintly)
- Debris: `Prop_Asteroid_Chunk_Small/Large`, scattered crystal ore (`Prop_OreNode_Crystal_*`, already shipped)
- Life returning: flora at the rim only. A wrecked `Prop_ArkWreck_Hull` sits on the slope, an ark that didn't make it.
- Gameplay: quest `q_crater` (inspect the core and get a lift_crystal), NPC `npc_crater_scholar`, lore `lore_impact`
- Audio ask (prometheus): low hum at the core
