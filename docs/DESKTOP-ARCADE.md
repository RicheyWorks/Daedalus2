# The Daedalus arcade

Eleven small games, with their own corners of Daedalus to explore.

| Game | Identity |
|---|---|
| **Twinwake Volley** | A fast paddle duel across the twinwake court. Keep the rally alive and send the next return past your rival. |
| **Dungeon Prototype** | Follow the Threadlight lantern circuit, light the room in the right order, and unlock the passage out. |
| **Driftstone Patrol** | Pilot the driftstone belt. Turn, thrust, and clear drifting rocks while watching the skies for trouble. |
| **Football / Soccer** | A ninety-second duel on the Daedalus pitch. Defend your goal, find an opening, and make the next kick count. |
| **Glowtrail Chase** | Gather the glowtrail through a winding maze. Evade the pursuers and use the power lights to turn the chase around. |
| **Riftwing Vanguard** | Push the rift frontier in a horizontal flight. Collect a changing arsenal and take on the frontier's heavy defender. |
| **Shadow Ninja** | Scale the ember terraces with wall moves and an air jump. Sword, throwing stars, and a ground spin prepare you for the sentinel. |
| **Star Shooter** | Hold the starlanes through escalating waves. Choose your difficulty, collect upgrades, and face changing boss patterns. |
| **Courtyard Clash** | Lead three teammates through four courtyard rounds. Charge a throw, make the catch, and keep your team standing. |
| **Ironwake Advance** | Run the ironwake route through platforms and enemy fire. Find weapon upgrades, keep moving, and cross the finish line. |
| **Lanternwild Quest** | Explore Lanternwild, defeat its guardians, and recover the key. Gather trinkets and hearts along the way. |

Existing controls, game rules, and visual styles carry each identity.
