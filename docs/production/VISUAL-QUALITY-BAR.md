# Visual quality bar — 14 October 2026

User decision: **Adopt Me is the minimum bar. Only our strongest finished work goes
into the metaverse.** Exceeding it is an objective, not an achievement established
by owning Blender or Unreal. This research defines the comparisons and delivery
evidence needed to make that claim credible.

## Research method and limits

Ten metaverse/social-world benchmarks were captured from public pages or their
published images, plus separate cozy-world, character and Bitcoin references.
This is a curated design sample, not a measured worldwide popularity or beauty
ranking. Platform content varies by creator. Historical images retain their
original context; a 2026 capture does not make a 2020 scene current gameplay.
Screenshots show appearance, not frame rate, responsiveness or animation quality.
No competitor client was installed, no wallet connected and no purchases made.

Reference screenshots and exact source metadata are in
`reference/visual-benchmark-20261014/manifest.json`; `index.html` displays the board.
These captures are research references, not models or textures for our game.

## Stronger supplementary environment and character targets

[Minecraft Vibrant Visuals](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/vibrant-visuals-update)
is an especially relevant voxel-lighting comparison. Its official mesa image
shows that block geometry can retain its identity while light, shadows, reflective
water and atmosphere improve the result. Our inference: expensive geometry is not
the first fix for our flat harbor; better terrain composition and light are.

[Animal Crossing: New Horizons](https://animalcrossing.nintendo.com/new-horizons/)
provides our cozy-character reference. In the captured official key art, species,
face shapes, clothing and poses remain identifiable among a busy group. Treat this
as a design benchmark, not as a copied character set. Our own unicorns, workers,
friendly plants and villagers need similarly recognizable roles and personalities.

[Palia's official fishing screenshot](https://palia.com/press) provides a stronger
environment target than a sparse prototype: layered trees, flowers, water, cliff
backdrop and a detailed dock frame a character interaction. Our dock should have
that sense of place rather than float over an undifferentiated grass disk.

[Roblox Dress To Impress](https://www.roblox.com/games/15101393044/Dress-To-Impress)
adds a separate avatar/clothing reference: a captured official-page video frame
shows the character beside a color-selection UI. Our realm-themed inventory must
keep the item preview, amount and selected state clear rather than cover the avatar
with decoration. This is appearance evidence, not a tested clothing system.

## Bitcoin collectibles: verified examples and useful distinctions

[BlockForge Identities](https://www.blockforgebtc.com/blockforge/identities) is a
direct voxel-character example. We opened an actual linked
[Identity #057 inscription](https://ordinals.com/content/9b44736ba8dc36b0d43ba3b86392a987df690df403b63032966782b040a50c59i56)
and captured its rendered character, biome stage and animation controls. A rich
interactive display is visibly possible; the project site's claims about all
100 pieces, game integration and networking were not independently audited.
The useful lesson is consistent character identity between collectible display
and the game, with a distinctive backdrop rather than a generic rarity frame.

[OCM Dimensions](https://onchainmonkey.com/collection/ocm-dimensions) is released
Bitcoin generative 3D art, not a voxel metaverse or proof of a full-body game rig.
The official site dates its launch to June 2023. We opened its linked
[Dimensions #215 viewer](https://ord.osura.com/content/2ea8e9c4454dff626a584c5da0b5db88f66b7efa39aabfcc6714fcbff695901ci0)
and captured the rendered sculpture. Its technical account describes shared
inscribed libraries and compression. Small per-piece data should not be confused
with the total size or cost of all dependencies.

[Bitmap](https://gitbook.bitmap.land/) is a Bitcoin land-claim standard, not one
universal finished visual world. Its mapping/ownership idea is separate from how
well any client renders the land. We should not cite a Bitmap identifier as a
graphics benchmark or infer hosting/persistence from an inscription.

A search also surfaced Block Frogs, but its current domain redirected to a domain
sale page. It is excluded as a verified current visual/release reference. Search
snippets alone are insufficient collection evidence.

[Ordinal recursion](https://docs.ordinals.com/inscriptions/recursion.html) permits
inscriptions to refer to other inscription content. The design implication for us
is to investigate shared/versioned visual definitions and deterministic identity,
while keeping ownership, authoritative gameplay, downloads and renderer budgets
explicit. No chain migration, fee estimate, mint or server deployment is proposed
as part of this visual study.

## Our current gap

The actual Copper Beacon revision-2 Blender render has readable buildings and a
useful palette, but remains below the adopted bar. The terrain is flat, the left
side is visually empty, flowers read as scattered dots, tree spacing lacks authored
groves, trees overlap the cottage, the waterfall is a rigid ribbon, and intersecting
dock boards create visible surface artifacts. Preserve it as an editable prototype;
do not count it as a finished metaverse island.

Producing hundreds of such variants would multiply these weaknesses. First finish
one hero environment, one friendly character, one enemy and one pet. Then reuse
their proven geometry, rig, materials and composition rules for the larger roster.

## Concrete delivery bar

| Area | Required before an asset is called ready | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Character identity | Distinct silhouette, face and costume; three meaningful shapes, coherent realm colors; recognizable at normal game camera distance | Front/side/back views, grayscale silhouette, in-world group view |
| Character movement | Idle, walk, run, start/stop, turns, jump/land and one role action; feet grounded, no visible sliding or abrupt clip snaps; eye/head attention and pet secondary motion | Continuous in-world clip, including transitions and slopes; stills alone cannot pass |
| Terrain composition | Main landmark, supporting clusters, varied elevation and a readable route; intentional quiet areas rather than accidental empty ground | Player-height route views plus island-wide view |
| Surface finish | No z-fighting, clipping, floating props, obviously stretched textures or conspicuous repetition; materials distinguish rock, wood, foliage, metal and water | Close and normal-distance views under day and dusk lighting |
| Water and atmosphere | Connected shoreline/stream/fall, controlled foam/ripples/reflections, depth layers; restrained rainbow dust consistent with user design | In-world movement clip and day/dusk stills |
| UI | Clear tabs, item pictures and quantities, selected state, legible contrast, realm-specific styling; preview does not compete with labels | Inventory at the intended display size with representative item quantities |
| Runtime | Declare target machine/resolution/settings and crowd size. Proposed first target: 60fps/16.7ms frame time on an agreed desktop, with scalable lower settings; not currently measured | Profile of the actual saved scene; distinguish GPU/CPU/load hitches from average FPS |
| Delivery identity | Source/export/import/placement/save stages tracked separately; character/world image reflects the shipped materials and rig | Hash manifest, native asset/map paths and owner handoff |

Art direction should remain distinct per realm: warm storybook adventure for the
Dragon Warrior-inspired realm; sea-glass blues and coral accents underwater;
charcoal/violet/warm lanterns in Labyrinth; approachable helpers amid the spooky
realm's threat; weathered practical materials in Hearthwake. Shared tabs and rig
contracts remain consistent while assets and finishes change.

## Blender-to-Unreal implementation priorities

First remove geometric defects and finish composition in Blender: layered terrain,
shoreline geometry, authored vegetation groups, fitted props, correct normals,
role-separated meshes and collision. Then establish one polished material/light
setup in Unreal and check imported scale and pivots. Do not replace original team
islands or duplicate owned generators.

For movement, author species-appropriate skeletons and transitions rather than
renaming a generic walk clip. Unreal [Blend Spaces](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/blend-spaces-in-unreal-engine?lang=en-US)
can blend locomotion from input parameters; our implementation still needs actual
native evaluation. Add contact-aware feet and restrained secondary motion only
after the basic movement works.

Lighting must fit the runtime budget. Epic's [Lumen performance guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/lumen-performance-guide-for-unreal-engine)
separates quality tiers and rendering costs; maximum settings are not a guarantee
of playable results. Preserve readability at the lower tier, use materials and
instancing deliberately, and measure the chosen scene rather than promise a
frame rate from an engine feature list.

Next production sequence: finish Copper Beacon composition; finish one original
unicorn or worker against Adopt Me/Animal Crossing references; prove locomotion
and role action in Unreal during the owner's application window; capture the
same views as the reference board; document remaining differences honestly.
