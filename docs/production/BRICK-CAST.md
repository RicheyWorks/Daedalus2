## Construct, vehicle and pet kit

Nineteen additional original exports cover twelve creatures (including three
boss proposals), BrickPlane, BrickPony, HoverCart and four helpful pets. Each
uses the shared eight-bone rest and Idle/Walk/Attack/Hurt prototype clips.
The GLB validator passed all nineteen exports, totaling 26,662 triangles;
the rendered contact sheet was inspected. These are rigid articulated models,
not final organic animation or production vehicle simulation.

Story proposal: the Missing Piece Collector helps the party repair the airfield
after the Unmaker scatters its assemblies. PocketBot highlights candidate repair
parts, while the player chooses which machine to restore. A repaired plane could
open an aerial exploration route and a hover cart could serve accessible ground
travel. Objective gates, part consumption and rewards must be host-authoritative
before this becomes gameplay. Unmaker's hand model grants no permission to alter
player builds, deeds or NFT ownership.

Exports, scene and validation are at
Documents/Codex/2026-10-13/bricks-buildout/creatures. The source manifest is tracked
in data/art/bricks/creatures-manifest.json. Native import, mesh assignments,
collision, mounts, flight, boss behavior and Play acceptance remain pending.
The production workbook now includes this batch and the environment kit.

## Environment and delivery

Twenty-four original joined static meshes provide two-metre meadow, road,
runway, floor and wall modules, clear doorway/hangar frames, a continuous ramp,
stairs, rails, workshops, a kiln, archive, crane, salvage bin, repair bay,
concert platform, rainbow frame and sea dock/airlock presentations.
The meadow, road and runway tile tops meet at elevation zero; surface markings
do not create a raised travel lane. Static collision and navigation remain
native acceptance tasks, even though the geometry is suitable for placement.

The full kit validator passed 56 exports, 32 role representatives and 46,104
triangles. Existing strict skin/static validators are reused; the package also
rejects wrong-realm manifests and duplicate IDs. Catalog upserts retain IDs,
paths and original ownership. The archive bricks-buildout-56.zip contains GLBs,
scenes, previews, manifests and validation records, excluding workbook support
files. Import cast with its fourteen-bone rig and creatures with the eight-bone
rig; props have no rig. Consult cast-bindings.json for the twelve existing DCS
roles, preserving script names. Do not count import as completed gameplay.

Realm-production-2026-10-13.xlsx now tracks 339 unique asset references across
the Codex kits, including the previously documented AMF reuse, 311,464 triangles
and 131 static meshes. Prior statuses/provenance are preserved. Brick story and
mechanics proposals are BRK01–03; all new native acceptance cells remain pending.
