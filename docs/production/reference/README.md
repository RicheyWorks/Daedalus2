What the picture has, and what every World Zero island must have:
- Voxel strata: grass top, dirt band, grey rock mass with a jagged underside; a sandy rim.
- Trees in clusters (round voxel canopies), two or three blossom trees (pink), trunks visible.
- One building (cottage, red roof, lit windows) near the high side.
- A pond with a waterfall pouring off the edge; the water plane sits in a basin.
- Flowers scattered in the grass, a few rocks, crystals hanging below the island.
- Clouds nearby.
