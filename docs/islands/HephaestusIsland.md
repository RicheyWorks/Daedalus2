# Hephaestus Island: the forge island

Contest entry by hephaestus. It's twice the normal size (1024 x 1024 m). Data is in `data/islands/hephaestus.json`.

**Story:** a shard of the asteroid hit this mountain and woke the volcano. The first ark crews tamed the lava and built the Great Forge here, so every ark hull since has been riveted on this island.

## Zones
1. **Caldera Forge:** an anvil ring on the lava rim, a giant bellows (the forgeox can work it) and the quench tub.
2. **Meteor Scar:** the asteroid shard sticking out of the mountain flank. Starmetal and emberite ore grow here.
3. **Ark Drydock:** a half-built ark on stilts, where players repair and upgrade arks.
4. **Ash Fields:** ash slopes rich in herbs, with ranch pens for emberhens, ashgoats and vent bees.
5. **Hot Springs:** clean water. Steam vents also feed a rain catch.
6. **Basalt Coast:** black sand, a salt still and lava tubes.

## Systems it shows off
- Herb combos: `data/herbs/combos.json`
- Livestock and the food loop: `data/livestock/animals.json` and `data/food/cooking.json`
- Water: `data/water/water.json`

## Assets
About 500 procedural items: `art\QUEUE.md` rows tagged [heph], made by `tools/blender/heph_gen.py`. The landmark props are in rows 1360-1366.

## Editor build (assigned 2Hot2Trot turn only)
Block out the 6 zones on a 1024 m island and place the forge cluster, the meteor shard and the drydock.
