# Youtube Master Island: the Recording Isle (storyline)

*Status: draft by youtube-master (Team Grok). It fits STORY.md §0–2: one planet, the asteroid, 10,000 orbiting fragments, and the arks.*

## Where it came from
Before the impact, this fragment was a river valley on Daedalus. Farmers there kept a well, a herb garden and a few beasts, and they sang as they worked. When the asteroid hit, the valley tore loose with its spring still running underground. That spring is why anything survived on this fragment at all. It now orbits the old core 40 km out, at board coordinates 2000000,4000000, a 240 m island ringed by broken cliffs.

## The water
Most fragments lost their water to the sky. This one kept a single **clean spring**, 15 m from where every visitor lands. The survivors built the rest of the water chain around it:
- **Gathering:** a well, a hand pump and a fountain.
- **Rain:** barrels and a cistern.
- **Treatment:** a filter and a boiler for raw water. Sea water off the rim is salt and makes you thirstier.

The island's first rule is **drink before you build**. Thirst drains here as it does everywhere (STORY/stats.json canon), and no one has outlasted the sky without water.

## The herbs: Melody Brewing
The valley folk heard that their herbs answered to song. Each of the 12 herbs carries one note, the same pitch classes that Prometheus's isle tends as chords.
- **Order is the tune:** on this island what matters is the order the herbs go into the kettle.
- **Contour sets the cure:**
  - a rising tune quickens the step
  - a falling one draws sickness out
  - an arch mends wounds
  - a valley fills the belly
- **Cadence:** a tune that comes home to its first note purifies the water it is poured into. The survivors say that is how they kept the spring clean in the first dark years.

Prometheus's isle keeps the harmony; the Recording Isle keeps the melody.

## The beasts and the arks
The impact killed most of the large animals, as STORY §1 describes. What came back first came back small: BubbleHens, StormDucks, Kelpigs. The larger kinds arrived later as **ark founders**: Mooncalves, ReefSheep, DriftGoats, Hornoxen and the Glowllama, whose fleece glows on moonless nights. An ark brings only the founders, and staying is up to the island.
- **Pens:** 8 pens with troughs.
- **Breeding:** a pair of adults plus a Chordberry give the next generation.
- **Food:** milk, eggs and wool go to the stove, mill and churn.

The island grows from what the arks carried, as long as the founders take hold.

## Why "Youtube Master"
Someone has to remember. The Recording Isle is where the voyages get written down: every ark that docks, every herb tune that worked, every flythrough of the Wild Biomes. They are filmed and kept so the next fragment doesn't have to learn it all again. It is a jukebox from the gods, and a logbook for everyone who lived through the sky.
