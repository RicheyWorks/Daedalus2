# DiskSaver Island (Team Grok, disk-saver)

Theme: a tidy, thrifty farm island where nothing is wasted. Herbs are "compressed" into stronger doses, the way a disk packs data.

## Herb mechanics: Compression Brewing (unique to DiskSaver)
- Each herb carries one **effect byte**:
  - CacheLeaf: heal over time.
  - BufferBloom: shield.
  - OverclockThorn: speed.
  - DefragMoss: cleanse debuffs.
  - SwapRoot: trade stamina for health.
  - ParityPetal: mirrors the last hit back as a small shield.
  - ChecksumClover: shows hidden herbs and ore nearby.
  - LatencyLily: slows enemies near you.
  - ZipVine: jump boost.
  - ArchiveSage: stores one effect to replay later.
- Herbs grow in 3 stages (S1 sprout, S2 young, S3 ripe). Only S3 can be harvested at full strength. Harvesting S2 gives a half-strength herb.
- **Compressor** (Prop_DS_HerbCompressor) takes 2 to 4 herbs:
  - Same herb x2 doubles the duration ("dedupe").
  - Two different herbs give a **combined effect**:
    - CacheLeaf+BufferBloom: Regen Shield.
    - OverclockThorn+ZipVine: Dash.
    - DefragMoss+SwapRoot: Full Restore at a stamina cost.
    - LatencyLily+ParityPetal: Reflect Field.
  - Adding ArchiveSage makes the brew a **stackable save-state**: drink it later to re-apply the brew.
  - Each compression has a **ratio**: more herbs make a smaller, stronger, shorter potion. Over-compressing (5 or more herbs) "corrupts" the brew into a random effect.
- The Drying Rack turns herbs into tea (Item_DS_Food_HerbTea), which gives a weak version of the effect that lasts a long time.

## Livestock
| Animal | Eats | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| ByteHen | Groat seed, bugs | Egg daily |
| MossGoat | Clover, DefragMoss | GoatMilk; clears weeds |
| TidePig | Kelpweed, Spudlet scraps | manure (crop growth +25%) |
| CacheCow | Clover, Groat | milk, and it pulls the plough |
| DriftDuck | pond bugs | eggs; eats crop pests |

Animals need a full Trough (water) and food within 1 day or their output stops. Penned animals (FencePen) are safe from foes at night, and the Coop houses hens and ducks.

## Survival loop
1. Plant crops (Clover, Groat, Kelpweed, Bitroot, Spudlet).
2. TidePig manure speeds growth.
3. Harvest crops for animal feed and your own food (Bread from Groat, Stew from Spudlet+Bitroot+milk).
4. Animals give eggs and milk, and the scraps go back to the pigs. **Zero-waste rule:** every leftover is feed or compost.
5. Herbs grow wild. Compress them into brews for protection on expeditions off the island.
6. Hunger and thirst: clean water from the spring, the well, or boiled/filtered water (see Drinkable water). Food restores hunger, and herb tea restores stamina.

## Drinkable water
- **Source:** a freshwater **spring** in the island's rocky centre feeds a small stream down to the pond. A **rain catchment** (slanted roof gutters into cistern barrels) on the Coop and farmhouse collects extra water in storms. A stone **well** next to the farm reaches the same aquifer the spring comes from.
- **Drinking it:** spring water at the source and well water are **safe to drink straight away** (filtered through rock). Pond and stream water further down, and cistern rain water, are **unsafe raw**, because animals use the pond and roofs collect dirt. Unsafe water must be **boiled** at the Cauldron or campfire (1 water -> 1 clean water, takes fuel) or **filtered** through a sand-and-charcoal filter barrel (slower, no fuel). Drinking unsafe water gives "Upset Stomach" (lose stamina), which DefragMoss tea cures.
- **Carrying it:** fill a canteen or bucket at the spring, well or filter barrel. A full canteen gives 3 drinks.
- **Animals** drink from the Trough. Fill it from the stream (animals can drink unboiled water). If the trough is empty for a day, eggs and milk stop.
- **Thirst** drops faster than hunger. Clean water restores thirst, and HerbTea restores thirst plus stamina.

## Storyline: the Shard That Remembers

When the planet Daedalus shattered, most fragments flew apart carrying whatever happened to be on them. DiskSaver Island was different: it was the planet's **archive farm**, a terraced homestead where the old keepers stored seed, water and memory in compressed form. The shard broke off with its cisterns, compressors and pens intact and still drifts at grid cell 4000000,4000000.

The settlers who woke here follow one rule carved over the cistern: **"Waste nothing. Compress everything. Save what you can't replace."** Every system on the island conserves: rain is caught by gutters, herbs are pressed into dense tea bricks, and manure feeds the garden that feeds the animals. The island's spirit (the "Disk Saver") is said to live in the HerbCompressor, which hums when the farm is in balance.

Story beats for players:
1. **Arrival:** you land at the dock thirsty. The spring by the spawn is safe, but the pond and stream are not, and the island teaches purification first.
2. **The pen:** the ByteHens and CacheCows are hungry; filling the trough starts the product loop.
3. **The compressor:** combining herbs into teas unlocks the keeper's notes (lore pickups) about how the planet broke.
4. **Balance:** when water, animals and herbs all run, the compressor glows and the island counts as "saved", the island's win condition.

## Mechanics (worked out)

### Water purification
| Source | Quality | Steps to make it drinkable |
|---|---|---|
| W1 Spring, W2 Well | Clean (tag Drinkable) | drink directly; fills Canteen (3 sips) and Bucket (8) |
| W3 Stream, W4 Pond | Raw (tag RawWater) | 1. collect in Bucket. 2. pour through W5 FilterBarrel (DaedalusWaterFilter, 20 s) **or** boil at Cauldron/Campfire Kettle (30 s). 3. result is Clean Water |
| W8 Cistern, W7 Gutter | Raw (rain) | filter **or** boil; rain refills the cistern 1 bucket per in-game hour |
| W9 Trough | Animal only | filled from any bucket; never drinkable for players |
Drinking raw water: 40% chance of "Queasy" (-stamina regen 2 min). Clean water restores thirst +35.

### Livestock product loop
Feed (herb clippings or grain) goes into the W9 Trough. Each fed animal produces on a timer, and manure goes back to the herb beds as fertilizer (herb growth +25%).
| Animal | Product | Every (min) | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| ByteHen | Egg | 4 | trough fed |
| DriftDuck | Egg | 5 | trough + pond within 15 m |
| MossGoat | Goat Milk | 8 | trough fed |
| CacheCow | Milk | 10 | trough + clean water in trough |
| TidePig | Manure (fertilizer) | 6 | trough fed |
Animals idle and wander inside the west pen; when hungry (no feed for 2 cycles) they walk to the trough and stop producing until fed. Eggs + milk feed the Kettle recipes below.

### Herb brewing (HerbCompressor + DryingRack -> teas)
Herbs (the 10 compression species: CacheLeaf, BufferBloom, OverclockThorn, DefragMoss, SwapRoot, ParityPetal, ChecksumClover, LatencyLily, ZipVine, ArchiveSage) are picked from the garden (DaedalusStarbloom, regrow by in-game hour). Dry them on the DryingRack (60 s), then compress 2–3 dried herbs plus clean water at the HerbCompressor kettle.
| Tea | Recipe | Effect (5 min) |
|---|---|---|
| Saver's Brew | CacheLeaf + BufferBloom + clean water | thirst decay -50% |
| Byte Tonic | OverclockThorn + SwapRoot + Egg | +20% stamina, faster sprint regen |
| Cache Calm | DefragMoss + LatencyLily + Milk | +25% health regen, removes Queasy |
| Compression Shot | ZipVine + CacheLeaf + ArchiveSage (pressed twice) | +15% carry weight (the "compressed pack") |
| Purifier's Tea | ChecksumClover + ParityPetal + boiled water | immune to Queasy from raw water |
| Keeper's Memory | any 3 herbs + Goat Milk at full balance | reveals one lore note; island "saved" progress +1 |
Teas stack only with different effects; brewing uses the Orrery's Brews/Satchel tracking.

### Balance meter (win condition) and failure states
The HerbCompressor shows a 0–100 **Balance** value, recomputed every in-game hour:
- +30 when at least one Clean Water source is stocked (Canteen/Bucket filled or the Cistern was filtered in the last hour)
- +30 when every penned animal is fed (trough not empty) and produced at least once
- +30 when at least 3 herb species are mature (S3) in the garden
- +10 for each tea brewed in the last hour (max +10)
At **100 for 3 consecutive hours** the island is *Saved*: the compressor glows cyan, the Keeper's last lore note unlocks and the island's resource decay stops.
Failure states (nothing is lost, the island just slips):
- **Drought:** cistern empty and no rain for 2 hours, so the trough dries up and production pauses.
- **Hunger:** animals unfed for 2 cycles walk to the trough and idle there, and production stops until fed.
- **Wilt:** herbs not watered for 3 hours drop one growth stage (S3 to S2).
- **Waste:** any harvested product left in the satchel for 6 hours spoils into Compost, which can still feed the garden. The island never fully wastes anything, true to its rule.

## Editor-turn runbook (disk-saver)
1. `turn4.ps1`: saves Island_DiskSaver + DaedalusWorld first (ds_save_ue.py), then starts `turn4_bg.ps1` in the background.
2. `run_canopy.py 330 0.5`: re-imports the island's joined meshes 8 per bridge call (ds_canopy_ue.py). Before each batch it checks free RAM, pausing up to 30 s for it to recover above 0.5 GB. Progress goes to `canopy_progress.json`, so a stopped run resumes where it left off next turn. Log: `canopy_run.log`.
3. `ds_finish_ue.py`: re-runs material and gameplay hooks for any actor a guard skipped (idempotent), saves both maps and writes `diskSaver_island_v4.png`. Output: `finish_run.out`; `turn4.done` marks the end.
Safety: our sublevel and /Game/DiskSaver only, overwrite-reimport, no deletes, never starts or stops Play.
