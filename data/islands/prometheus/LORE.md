Prometheus Island was grown, not built: its ridges and gorges come from a single Turing reaction-diffusion seed, and a spring at the summit feeds the stream that cuts through them. At its heart a jukebox keeps the island song, and twelve herb beds ring the garden on the circle of fifths, so a major triad heals, a fifth shields and a tritone poisons. The livestock wear coats grown from the same equations, and each one sings its genome as a call.
